# Eventer privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026. Maintainer: [bobuel](https://github.com/bobuel).

Eventer is a collection of local skills and planning scripts. It has no Eventer
account, hosted backend, analytics, telemetry or publisher-operated data store.
The maintainer does not receive event records through the plugin. Your AI harness,
model provider and any connected tools process information under their own terms,
privacy policies and account settings. Local storage does not mean that prompts
or files read by your harness stay on your computer.

## Information used and why

When you choose to provide it, Eventer uses event dates and locations, guest names
and contact details, RSVP replies, household authority, travel windows, rooming
and meal preferences, vendor quotes, reported payment amounts, message receipts
and host decisions. It uses these records to prepare plans, reconcile changes,
calculate requirements and balances, and help you review communications.

Accommodation needs can reveal sensitive personal information. Collect only the
minimum practical requirement needed for the event, explain its purpose and
intended recipients, and obtain the guest's appropriate consent before collecting
or sharing it. Guests may decline to provide it and coordinate privately with the
host or venue instead. Do not supply medical records, diagnoses, government IDs,
card or bank account details, passwords, API keys or authentication tokens. Eventer
does not provide medical advice or certify allergen safety.

## Who can receive information

Your harness and model provider may receive the prompts, files and tool results
you make available. A connected email, calendar, search or other provider receives
information only through the tools used in your authorized task. Guests and vendors
receive the content you authorize for their exact audience. General guest updates
should exclude individual accommodation, allergy, rooming and private travel details.
Review tool permissions and messages before granting external-action authority.

The maintainer receives information you choose to post in public GitHub issues or
send through an agreed support channel. Do not post guest records or credentials.
GitHub processes repository visits and contributions under its own privacy policy.
The plugin does not sell event data or use it for advertising.

## Retention and your controls

Event files remain in the folder you choose until you remove them. There is no
automatic expiry. Export makes additional copies; choosing a retention period
records your choice and does not itself delete anything. Uninstalling the plugin
leaves event files, exports and external schedules intact.

You can inspect, correct, export or delete your local records; restrict filesystem
access; revoke connected-tool permissions; and cancel authorized schedules through
the harness that owns them. Ask before deleting records needed for unresolved
commitments. Backups, model-provider records and external messages have their own
retention and deletion controls; Eventer cannot guarantee their deletion.

## Questions and changes

For general questions, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/bobuel/eventer-plugin/issues)
without personal event information. For a sensitive concern, request a private
contact route without including the sensitive material. Changes to this policy
are published in this repository with an updated effective date.
